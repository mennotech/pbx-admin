# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and productive project community. Participants are expected to:

- communicate with empathy and technical clarity;
- welcome constructive feedback and different levels of experience;
- focus criticism on ideas and behavior, not people;
- respect privacy and avoid sharing another person's identifying or operational information;
- accept maintainer decisions gracefully, including decisions about scope and support.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, doxxing, and publishing private communications or security details without permission.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other spaces where someone is representing this project or its community.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock threads, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this policy. Enforcement decisions will consider context, impact, and patterns of behavior.

Report conduct concerns privately through the contact methods on the [Mennotech GitHub profile](https://github.com/mennotech). Do not open a public issue containing sensitive personal details. Reports will be reviewed as privately and promptly as maintainer capacity allows. Retaliation against a reporter is prohibited.
